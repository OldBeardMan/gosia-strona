Małgorzata Śledź

Energy engineer · solar PV, wind, battery storage

I studied energy engineering at AGH University in Kraków and have been working on renewable energy projects since 2021. I started at Alpha Solar (now part of RWE), running yield analyses for PV farms bidding in the Polish renewable auctions. Then I spent three years at KENO managing EPC solar projects in Poland and several other EU countries. Now at Hynfra I work on renewable and battery storage projects in the Middle East and Africa.

Outside the energy sector I've done some work on CO₂ emissions reporting and ESG strategy. For the past nine years I've also been tutoring maths, geography and English, preparing students for their final school exams.
